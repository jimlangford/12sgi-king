Bill 9 — the vote, the money, and the question

Bill 9 (2025) phases out transient vacation-rental use in apartment (A-1/A-2) districts — returning housing to residents (2029 West Maui, 2031 elsewhere). Passed the Maui County Council on second and final reading and was signed into law.
