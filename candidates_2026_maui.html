2026 Maui Candidates — Who Ran, and Who Funds Them

The general election was held on 2026-11-03. This page lists everyone who filed for Mayor and the nine County Council seats, with the campaign money reported for the 2026 cycle and the results that have a cited source.

Money is the 2026-cycle total reported to the Hawaii Campaign Spending Commission (CSC), not a candidate's career total, and it is only what has been filed. “RE” is the real-estate / development signal from self-reported employer or occupation, a lead and not a finding. A name is matched on surname and first name; where a filer shares a surname with a different person, that money is NOT counted. “No money matched” means nothing was found under that name, not that nothing was raised. Money is lawful; it is shown beside each name as a question, not an accusation.

Results marked uncertified are election-night counts from the news sources linked; they are replaced by the Office of Elections certified results when those are posted.
