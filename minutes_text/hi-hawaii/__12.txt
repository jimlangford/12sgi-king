ORDER OF RESOLUTIONS 
 
Res. 645-26: 
 
AUTHORIZES THE PAYMENT OF FUNDS OF LATER FISCAL YEARS  
TO ENTER INTO A MULTI-YEAR AGREEMENT WITH JUST APPRAISED 
INC. TO RENEW A SOFTWARE LICENSE FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF 
FINANCE, REAL PROPERTY TAX DIVISION      
Authorizes the Mayor to enter into a three-year agreement with an approximate 
annual cost of $61,110, to be used by the department to update ownership 
information that is used for issuance of assessments, tax billing, and other required 
correspondences.   
 
Reference: Comm. 1019 
Intr. by: Council Member Kānealiʻi-Kleinfelder (B/R)  
 
 
Res. 646-26: 
 
AUTHORIZES THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN INTERGOVERNMENTAL 
AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE OF HAWAIʻI, DEPARTMENT OF THE 
ATTORNEY GENERAL, TO RECEIVE A GRANT FOR TRIAL ADVOCACY 
TRAINING           
Allows for the receipt of $86,932 in federally-derived funds to support trial 
advocacy training efforts to deputy prosecuting attorneys.  
 
Reference: Comm. 1020 
Intr. by: Council Member Kānealiʻi-Kleinfelder (B/R) 
Companion: Bill 182  
 
 
 
Res. 651-26: 
 
AUTHORIZES THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN INTERGOVERNMENTAL 
AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE OF HAWAIʻI, DEPARTMENT OF 
HEALTH, TO RECEIVE A GRANT FOR THE HAWAIʻI FIRE DEPARTMENT 
COMMUNITY PARAMEDICINE PROGRAM      
Allows for the receipt of $2,355,000 in federally-derived funds to be used to fund 
personnel, telehealth equipment and software, and provide training, travel, and 
program implementation materials.  
 
Reference: Comm. 1030 
Intr. by: Council Member Kānealiʻi-Kleinfelder (B/R)  
Companion: Bill 188

BILLS FOR ORDINANCES 
 
Bill 182: AMENDS ORDINANCE NO. 26-44, THE OPERATING BUDGET OF THE 
COUNTY OF HAWAIʻI FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING JUNE 30, 2027  
Appropriates revenues in the Federal Grants – Justice Assistant Grant account 
($86,932) and appropriates the same to the Justice Assistance Grant Program 
account.  Funds will be used to support trial advocacy training efforts to deputy 
prosecuting attorneys.  
 
Reference: Comm. 1021 
Intr. by: Council Member Kānealiʻi-Kleinfelder (B/R) 
Companion:  Resolution 646-26 
 
Bill 188: AMENDS ORDINANCE NO. 26-44, THE OPERATING BUDGET OF THE 
COUNTY OF HAWAIʻI FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING JUNE 30, 2027  
Appropriates revenues in the Federal Grants – Rural Health Transformation 
Program account ($2,355,000) and appropriates the same to the Rural Health 
Transformation Program account.  Funds will be used to fund personnel,  
telehealth equipment and software, and provide training, travel, and program 
implementation materials.  
 
Reference: Comm. 1030 
Intr. by: Council Member Kānealiʻi-Kleinfelder (B/R) 
Companion: Resolution 651-26 
 
 
Bill 190: AMENDS ORDINANCE NO. 26-44, THE OPERATING BUDGET OF THE 
COUNTY OF HAWAIʻI FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING JUNE 30, 2027  
Appropriates revenues in the Federal Grants – Workforce Innovation and 
Opportunity Act account ($1,300,000) and appropriates the same to the following 
Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act 2026-2027 accounts:  
Administrative/Planning ($150,000), Adult Program ($500,000), Dislocated Worker 
Program ($250,000), and Youth Program ($400,000).  Funds would be used to 
provide services that enhance employment opportunities, job retention, earnings, 
skill development and literacy. 
 
Reference: Comm. 1054 
Intr. by: Council Member Kānealiʻi-Kleinfelder (B/R) 
Companion: Resolution 666-26
