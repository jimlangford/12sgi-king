AUTHORIZES THE DIRECTOR OF FINANCE TO ENTER INTO 
NEGOTIATIONS FOR THE ACQUISITION OF LANDS OR 
CONSERVATION EASEMENTS FOR ALL OR A PORTION OF THE 
PROPERTIES IDENTIFIED AS TAX MAP KEYS:  1-5-088:009, 010, 011,  
012, AND 013, ALSO KNOWN AS WAIAKAHIʻULA VILLAGE OCEAN 
ACCESS, AND TAX MAP KEYS:  1-5-063:042 AND 043, ALSO KNOWN  
AS WAIAKAHIʻULA BURIAL SITE 19014 AND AGRICULTURAL

WAIAKAHIʻULA, DISTRICT OF PUNA, USING THE PUBLIC ACCESS, 
OPEN SPACE, AND NATURAL RESOURCES PRESERVATION FUND

fifth and sixth highest priorities in the 2025 Annual Report of the Public
