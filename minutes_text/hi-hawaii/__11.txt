ORDER OF RESOLUTIONS 
 
Res. 664-26: APPROVES THE HAWAIʻI COUNTY COUNCIL LEGISLATIVE PROPOSALS 
AND PRIORITIES TO BE CONSIDERED FOR INCLUSION IN THE 2027 
HAWAIʻI STATE ASSOCIATION OF COUNTIES LEGISLATIVE PACKAGE  
Includes proposals prohibiting the capture and sale of aquatic life for commercial 
aquarium purposes, allowing counties to request a state land use boundary 
amendment from agricultural to rural districts for small parcels, requiring 
denitrification in individual wastewater systems, extending the county surcharge of 
general excise tax until 2045, and updating eligible criteria for housing assistance 
applicants to allocate a percentage of housing units to be used by State or County 
employees under chapter 201H; and seven policy priorities relating to:  (1) 
herbicide usage on public property; (2) solar system installation on public property 
and renewable energy generation; (3) watershed management, riparian areas, and 
flood control; (4) mental and behavioral health resources; (5) economic 
development and diversification; (6) wastewater infrastructure and cesspool 
conversion; and (7) implementation of safe routes to school programs. 
 
 Reference: Comm. 1048 
 Intr. by: Council Member Kimball
