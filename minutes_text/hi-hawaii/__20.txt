Bill 168: REPEALS ORDINANCE NOS. 19-38, 09-50, 09-49, 06-138, 06-137, 95-118, 
92-36, 90-10, 86-49, 533 (1980), AND 594 (1973) AND AMENDS CHAPTER 25, 
ARTICLE 8, SECTION 25-8-3 (NORTH KONA ZONING MAP) AND SECTION 
25-8-5 (KAILUA URBAN ZONING MAP), OF THE HAWAI‘I COUNTY CODE 
1983 (2016 EDITION, AS AMENDED), BY CHANGING THE DISTRICT 
CLASSIFICATION FROM VILLAGE COMMERCIAL – 7,500 SQUARE FEET 
(CV-7.5) AND MULTIPLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL – 2,500 SQUARE FEET 
(RM-2.5) TO DOUBLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL – 3,750 SQUARE FEET 
(RD-3.75) AND AGRICULTURAL – FIVE ACRES (A-5A) AT HIENALOLI 
4TH, 5TH, AND 6TH, NORTH KONA, HAWAIʻI, COVERED BY TAX MAP 
KEY:  7-5-010:013 (Planning Director Initiated) (Area:  6.467 acres)  
The Leeward Planning Commission forwards its favorable recommendation to 
revert the current zoning of 3.331 acres from Village Commercial to Double-
Family Residential and 3.227 acres from Multiple-Family Residential to Unplanned 
zoning district.  This property is located on the north side of Hualālai Road, 
approximately 1,000 feet east of its intersection with Kuakini Highway, Hienaloli 
4th and 5th, North Kona. 
 
Reference: Comm. 937 
Intr. by: Council Member Kimball (B/R) 
Approve: LAAC-43 
 
; and 
Comm. 937.2: From Council Member Heather L. Kimball, dated July 7, 2026, transmitting 
proposed amendments to Bill 168.

Bill 165: 
(Draft 2) 
INITIATES AN AMENDMENT TO ARTICLE X OF THE CHARTER OF THE 
COUNTY OF HAWAI‘I (2024 EDITION), RELATING TO PUBLIC ACCESS, 
OPEN SPACE, AND NATURAL RESOURCES PRESERVATION  
Removes the $3 million accrual limit for the Public Access, Open Space, and 
Natural Resources Preservation maintenance fund; provides that all expenses from

from the fund.
