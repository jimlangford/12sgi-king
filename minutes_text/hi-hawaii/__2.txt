BILLS FOR ORDINANCES 
 
Bill 183: 
 
AMENDS CHAPTER 21, ARTICLES 1 AND 4, OF THE HAWAIʻI COUNTY 
CODE 1983 (2016 EDITION, AS AMENDED), RELATING TO SEWER 
SERVICE CHARGES         
Increases wastewater service rates and establishes a new service class with new 
service rates for hotels.  
 
Reference: Comm. 1022 
Intr. by: Council Member Villegas (B/R) 
Postponed: September 1, 2026 
 
(Note:  There is a motion by Council Member Hustace, seconded by Council 
Member Galimba, to recommend passage of Bill 183 on first reading.)  
 
 
 
Bill 191: 
 
AMENDS CHAPTER 10, ARTICLE 1, SECTION 10-8 OF THE HAWAIʻI 
COUNTY CODE 1983 (2016 EDITION, AS AMENDED), RELATING TO 
EROSION AND SEDIMENTATION CONTROL      
Establishes administrative enforcement provisions for violations of Chapter 10, 
including increased fines of up to $1,000 per day. 
 
Reference: Comm. 1058 
Intr. by: Council Member Inaba
