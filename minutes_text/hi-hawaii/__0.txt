Res. 664-26: 
(Draft 2) 
 
APPROVES THE HAWAIʻI COUNTY COUNCIL LEGISLATIVE PROPOSALS 
AND PRIORITIES TO BE CONSIDERED FOR INCLUSION IN THE 2027 
HAWAIʻI STATE ASSOCIATION OF COUNTIES LEGISLATIVE PACKAGE  
Includes proposals prohibiting the capture and sale of aquatic life for commercial 
aquarium purposes, allowing counties to request a state land use boundary 
amendment from agricultural to rural districts for small parcels, requiring 
denitrification in individual wastewater systems, extending the county surcharge of 
general excise tax until 2045, updating eligible criteria for housing assistance 
applicants to allocate a percentage of housing units to be used by State or County 
employees under chapter 201H, and allowing counties to regulate the sale of 
tobacco products; and ten policy priorities relating to:  (1) herbicide usage on public 
property; (2) solar system installation on public property and renewable energy 
generation; (3) watershed management, riparian areas, and flood control; (4) mental 
and behavioral health resources; (5) economic development and diversification; (6) 
wastewater infrastructure and cesspool conversion; (7) implementation of safe 
routes to school programs; (8) increasing funds for emergency preparedness, 
evacuation routes, notification systems, and community-level emergency planning; 
(9) responsible game management of wild ungulates; and (10) increasing wages. 
 
Reference: Comm. 1048.2 
Intr. by: Council Member Kimball 
Approve: GOEAC-163
