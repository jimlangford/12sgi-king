BILLS FOR ORDINANCES 
 
Bill 171: 
 
AMENDS CHAPTER 2, ARTICLE 4, SECTION 2-12.7, OF THE HAWAIʻI 
COUNTY CODE 1983 (2016 EDITION, AS AMENDED), RELATING TO 
FISCAL IMPACT STATEMENTS        
Requires that a fiscal impact statement prepared by the finance director be 
submitted with Council bills that have fiscal implications. 
 
Reference: Comm. 956 
Intr. by: Council Member Onishi 
Postponed: July 22, 2026 
 
(Note:  There is a motion by Council Member Onishi, seconded by Council 
Member Galimba, to recommend passage of Bill 171 on first reading.)

Bill 192: AMENDS CHAPTER 19, ARTICLE 11, SECTION 19-90 OF THE HAWAIʻI 
COUNTY CODE 1983 (2016 EDITION, AS AMENDED), RELATING TO  
REAL PROPERTY TAX         
Extends the requirement to appropriate residential tier-two and tier-three property 
tax revenue to the housing and homelessness fund until 2029; requires that monies 
from the fund be used to award grants that address housing and homelessness. 
 
Reference: Comm. 1059 
Intr. by: Council Member Inaba
