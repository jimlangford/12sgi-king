ORDER OF RESOLUTIONS 
 
Res. 602-26: AUTHORIZES THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN INTERGOVERNMENTAL  
AGREEMENT WITH THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF THE 
INTERIOR, NATIONAL PARK SERVICE, FOR INTERAGENCY PUBLIC 
SAFETY COORDINATION  
Establishes a working relationship between the National Park Service and Hawaiʻi 
Fire Department to allow for the coordination of personnel, services, equipment, 
and facilities required for the protection of life and properties during emergency 
incidents. 
 
Reference: Comm. 955 
Intr. by: Council Member Kagiwada (B/R)

Res. 603-26: URGES THE DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND BIOSECURITY TO 
IMPLEMENT AND ENFORCE PLANT QUARANTINE INTERIM RULE 26-1,  
REGULATING THE TRANPORTATION OF COCONUT RHINOCEROS 
BEETLE HOST MATERIAL ON HAWAIʻI ISLAND     
 
Reference:  Comm. 958 
Intr. by: Council Members Hustace and Inaba  
 
 
Res. 605-26: DIRECTS THE COUNTY CLERK TO TAKE NECESSARY ACTION TO  
PLACE THE CHARTER AMENDMENT PROPOSED BY ORDINANCE  
NO. 26-47, RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF THE COUNTY AUDITOR, ON 
THE 2026 GENERAL ELECTION BALLOT      
Proposes to clarify that the County Auditor has the same power with respect to 
employees within the Office of the County Auditor as department heads in the 
Executive Branch and specifies that the County Auditor may appoint a deputy.  
 
Reference:  Comm. 962 
Intr. by: Council Member Inaba
